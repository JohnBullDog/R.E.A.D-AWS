Practice a classroom decision

Pick a learning objective. R.E.A.D. finds the research for it and writes a classroom moment that turns on a real decision. You write what you would do, and your response is read back against the same research, point by point. Nothing is scored.

Scenarios are machine-written. Each one is generated when you ask for it, from the research sections shown under it, using worked examples written for development. No reading specialist has reviewed this scenario, so read it as practice, not as guidance.
Development objectives. The objective list and the worked examples are placeholders; Addison will rewrite them before any teacher use.

2. What would you do?

Write what you would do next and why, the way you would explain it to a coach. A short paragraph is enough.

Feedback

R.E.A.D. worked out what the research says to do at this decision point, then read your response against each of those points. The quotes in grey are your own words, copied exactly; the quotes in a research card are the source's own words.
