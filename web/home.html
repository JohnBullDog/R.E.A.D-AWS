Welcome to R.E.A.D.

The Reading Educator Assistance Desk gives K–5 educators answers and feedback grounded in Science of Reading research, with every source shown word for word.

Preview. R.E.A.D. is a proof of concept. Its answers are advisory: read the cited research and use your professional judgment.

How to use it

1. Ask or upload

Type a question as you would ask a colleague, or upload material and confirm the goal R.E.A.D. reads from it (grade band, focus, objective).

2. Check the evidence

Click a citation number to read the exact passage from the source. Quotes are copied from the research by code, never written by the AI, and checked against the original.

3. Use your judgment

Each answer shows how strong the evidence is (strong, limited, mixed, or contested) and when the research library was last updated. Treat it as a starting point, not a ruling.

Good to know

  • Only the research library. Answers come only from the openly licensed sources listed on the Sources page. If they don't cover a question, R.E.A.D. says so.
  • No student or teacher data. Upload sample material only. Uploaded material is never added to the library and is deleted after 24 hours.
  • The review checklist is a draft. Its questions are placeholders until the project's reading specialist rewrites them.
